01 / Flow

Press play. We handle the rest.

No upload step, no transcription dashboard, no second player. The experience stays where the media already is.

Play anything

Open local media, network video, or a livestream in VLC as you normally would.

Audio stays local

VAD and Whisper inference run on-device, accelerated through Vulkan when available.

Captions appear in VLC

Timed subtitles render in the same player and remain stable through pause, seek, and resume.

02 / Product

Built like a media tool, not an AI dashboard.

The visual system borrows from instrument panels and desktop software: restrained surfaces, clear state, and orange only where attention is useful.

03 / Privacy

Your movie never leaves your computer.

Speech detection and transcription run on your device. If you opt into translation, only finalized subtitle text is sent for translation — never raw audio.

Audio stays inside the local inference pipeline from capture through speech detection, transcription, and caption rendering.
